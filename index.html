⚙️ How QR-VISION Works

1️⃣ Scan
Scan the QR code attached to the vehicle number plate.
2️⃣ Identify
The QR code connects to the vehicle's digital profile.
3️⃣ Access Information
Important vehicle and emergency information can be viewed.
4️⃣ Connect
The system can provide a digital way to connect relevant people during emergencies.

💡 About the Project

QR-VISION is a prototype concept that connects a physical vehicle number plate with a digital information system. By scanning the QR code, users can access a digital vehicle profile instead of relying only on the physical number plate.


The prototype demonstrates how QR technology can be combined with future technologies such as GPS, IoT and Artificial Intelligence to create smarter and safer transportation systems.
