Past → Present → Future

PAST — Physical Identification

Vehicle identification depended mainly on the physical number plate and manual access to records.

PRESENT — QR Digital Identity

Our prototype adds a QR code to the physical plate. Scanning it opens a digital vehicle profile.

FUTURE — Connected Vehicle Identity

The concept could evolve by combining QR, NFC, IoT sensors, GPS, AI-based event detection, automated alerts and smart-city infrastructure.

About The Prototype

QR-VISION is a science-project prototype demonstrating how QR technology can create a digital identity layer for a physical vehicle number plate.


The current demonstration consists of a physical prototype plate, QR code and web-based vehicle profile.


GPS tracking, AI accident detection, IoT sensing, emergency automation and smart-city integration are future concepts, not active functions of this prototype.


All information shown on this demonstration website is fictional.
